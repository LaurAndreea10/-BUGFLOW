# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BUGFLOW a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, ability, experience, or education.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy and respect.
- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Respect accessibility needs and different ways of participating.
- Focus discussion on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
